## Health-adjacent chains (clinical first)

- **Incense smoke**: solid clinical record of harm. American Lung Association: ~1 g incense ≈ 45 mg particulate matter, ~4× a cigarette; PM2.5 reaches deep airways; long-term exposure lowers lung function. PMC8548258: review of adverse effects incl. respiratory, cardiovascular, cognitive (2020 cohort: weekly indoor burning, accelerated cognitive decline in older adults). Book framing already honest: the smell thinned but never cleared; the smoke is a tool with a price — ventilate, don't burn indoors daily, never in a closed room.
- **Candles**: soot and PM peaks during extinguishing (PMC11933706); EPA 2000: soot emissions rise after wax breakthrough. The burn lane therefore: outdoors, steel container.
- **Sleep paralysis**: Cleveland Clinic (my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/21974-sleep-paralysis): occurs between sleep and wakefulness, body atonic while conscious; parasomnias page separate. Used in ch3's honesty (mechanism named, lived account kept). The book already names the mechanism without hiding the lived layer.
- **Magnets**: NCCIH — static magnets not conclusively supported for pain; PEMF distinct. Book honesty: the magnetic absorption is lived report (his war, told as lived), not a clinical claim; the book never sells magnets as medicine.
- **Spiritual baths**: folk tradition documented (limpia/curanderismo); clinical caution: herbal baths can irritate skin; nothing ingested; keep out of reach of children; not a replacement for medical care.
