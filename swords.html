I · The sword you strap on

The armor piece. The sword of the Spirit is not a weapon you hold; it is the word you carry, and it is the only blade listed in the whole armor of God.

And take unto you the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit (which is the word of God). Ephesians 6:17

II · What the sword does when it lands

It does not cut flesh. It divides soul and spirit, joints and marrow — and it judges the thoughts and intents of the heart. The sharpest blade in the canon cuts intent, not just action.

For the word of God is living and effectual, and more piercing than any two edged sword; and reaching unto the division of the soul and the spirit, of the joints also and the marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart. Hebrews 4:12

III · The sword out of the mouth

The Word does not carry the sword. The Word is the sword, and it comes out of the mouth — in the prophet's calling, in the vision of the Son of man, and in the judgment of the nations.

And he hath made my mouth like a sharp sword: in the shadow of his hand he hath protected me, and hath made me as a chosen arrow: in his quiver he hath hidden me. Isaiah 49:2
And he had in his right hand seven stars. And from his mouth came out a sharp two edged sword: and his face was as the sun shineth in his power. Revelation 1:16
And out of his mouth proceedeth a sharp two edged sword; that with it he may strike the nations. And he shall rule them with a rod of iron; and he treadeth the winepress of the fierceness of the wrath of God the Almighty. Revelation 19:15

IV · What Jesus said about swords

Three teachings, exactly. The sword he brings is not metal — it is the cut between truth and lie. The swords the disciples bought were for show; he never drew one, and when Peter's blade cut an ear, he healed it. The hand-held sword is a trap: all that take the sword shall perish with the sword.

Do not think that I came to send peace upon earth: I came not to send peace, but the sword. Matthew 10:34
Then Jesus saith to him: Put up again thy sword into its place: for all that take the sword shall perish with the sword. Matthew 26:52
But they said: Nothing. Then said he unto them: But now he that hath a purse, let him take it, and likewise a scrip; and he that hath not, let him sell his coat, and buy a sword. Luke 22:36
But they said: Lord, behold here are two swords. And he said to them, It is enough. Luke 22:38
But Jesus answering, said: Suffer ye thus far. And when he had touched his ear, he healed him. Luke 22:51
The doctrine in one arc: never the hand-held sword, the sword that divides truth from lie, and the only real blade is the word out of the mouth. That is what a devil-killer is armed with.
